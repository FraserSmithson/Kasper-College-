Partner with Kasper College: Innovation, Talent & Enterprise Solutions

Industry Partnerships, Custom Corporate Training, Consultancy, Graduate Recruitment & Research Collaborations

1. Executive Introduction & Partnership Philosophy

At Kasper College, we firmly believe in the transformative power of collaboration between higher education and industry. Our college offers a comprehensive ecosystem of enterprise services engineered to accelerate business growth, bridge technical skills shortages, and drive forward-looking innovation across computing, cybersecurity, digital media, and product design.

Whether you are an established multinational tech enterprise, a scaling regional software company, or an ambitious creative startup, Kasper College provides direct access to cutting-edge research facilities, academic thought leadership, and an exceptional pipeline of work-ready talent.

1. Custom Corporate Training & Continuous Professional Development (CPD)

Upskill and empower your workforce with industry-accredited technical training tailored precisely to your strategic tech stack and organizational needs.

  • Customizable Modules: Executive cybersecurity awareness, cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure), full-stack modern web engineering, data analytics, AI & machine learning deployment, and UI/UX design systems.
  • Flexible Delivery Modes: On-site corporate masterclasses, evening executive sprints, fully remote online modules, and hybrid blended cohorts.
  • Certification: Accredited CPD certifications and micro-credentials issued upon module completion.

2. Research & Development (R&D) Collaborations

Leverage the expertise of our academic researchers, PhD candidates, and advanced computing laboratories to solve complex engineering challenges and accelerate product development.

  • Knowledge Transfer Partnerships (KTP): Government-subsidized partnerships embedding a high-caliber graduate specialist directly inside your business supervised by senior Kasper College faculty.
  • Proof-of-Concept & Prototyping: Rapid development of minimum viable products (MVPs), hardware prototypes, and algorithmic proofs using our 3D fabrication labs and GPU clusters.
  • Grant Application Assistance: Collaborative bidding for Innovate UK, EPSRC, and Horizon Europe technical research grants.

3. Design & Technology Consultancy

Access impartial, expert technical advice from our faculty specialists and senior industry fellows across diverse domains:

  • Cybersecurity & Penetration Audits: Vulnerability assessments, web application penetration tests, ISO 27001 readiness checks, and digital forensics.
  • Software & Cloud Architecture: Scalability reviews, legacy modernization strategies, database optimization, and microservice transition.
  • User Experience (UX) & Design Systems: Comprehensive usability audits, user testing laboratories, eye-tracking analytics, and responsive design systems.
  • Product & Spatial Design: Ergonomic prototyping, interior architecture modeling, and sustainable material specification.
