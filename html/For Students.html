Explore Kasper College: World-Class Facilities, Vibrant Student Life, and Support for Your Success

A Comprehensive Student Guide to Campus Resources, Academic Support, Student Union, Careers, and Living in Sheffield

At Kasper College, we are committed to providing our students with an inspiring, supportive, and cutting-edge environment to learn, create, innovate, and thrive. Our state-of-the-art campus in Sheffield has been purposefully designed to cater to the demands of modern computing, creative design, digital media, and networking disciplines. Whether you are building distributed systems, analyzing cyber threats, crafting immersive video games, or designing sustainable interior spaces, Kasper College provides the infrastructure and community you need to excel.

2. Comprehensive Student Support & Wellbeing

At Kasper College, we are committed to ensuring you receive all the pastoral and academic guidance you need throughout your university journey:

  • Academic Advising & Personal Tutors: Every student is assigned a dedicated Personal Tutor from their department for regular 1-to-1 academic progress reviews and mentoring.
  • Mental Health & Counseling Support:Free, confidential counseling sessions, mindfulness workshops, and crisis drop-in support available both in-person and online.
  • Disability, Accessibility & Neurodiversity Service:Tailored support plans, assistive software licenses, exam adjustments, and one-on-one specialist study skills tutors.
  • Student Finance & Emergency Hardship Fund:Expert advice on student loans, budgeting, scholarships, bursaries, and short-term interest-free emergency hardship assistance.

3. Kasper College Students' Union (KCSU) & Campus Life

The Kasper College Students' Union (KCSU) is run by students, for students, representing your voice and organizing a rich calendar of social, academic, and recreational events:

  • Student Societies: Kasper Computing Society (KCS), Cyber Defense Guild, Game Developers Circle, Creative Design Collective, Women in Tech Network, Robotics League, Esports Association, and Outdoor Adventure Club.
  • Annual Hackathons & Game Jams: The annual 48-hour Kasper Winter Hackathon brings together over 200 student coders and designers with prizes sponsored by leading tech firms
  • Student Representation: Course and Department Reps elected each term work closely with faculty heads to drive continuous course improvements.

4. Career Development & Employability Services

Our dedicated Careers & Employability Centre helps you turn your degree into a thriving career:

  • 1-to-1 Career Coaching: CV reviews, LinkedIn profiling, portfolio curation, and mock technical coding interviews.
  • Placement Support: Dedicated placement officers help you secure 12-month paid industrial placements and summer internships.
  • Kasper Tech & Creative Careers Fair: Held every November in the Glass Atrium, featuring 40+ employers recruiting directly for graduate positions.
  • Alumni Mentoring Network: Connect with Kasper College alumni working at leading tech companies, gaming studios, and design consultancies worldwide.

5. Digital Campus Services & IT Support for Students

  • Campus Wi-Fi: Seamless, high-speed Eduroam wireless coverage across all campus areas and student residences.
  • Free Software Licenses: All registered students receive free Microsoft 365, GitHub Student Developer Pack, JetBrains IDE licenses, Autodesk suite, and MATLAB.
  • Virtual Learning Environment (VLE): 24/7 access to lecture recordings, course slides, assignment submission portals, and grades via Moodle.
  • Cloud Compute & Printing Credits: Annual allowance of £30 free cloud compute credits and 500 pages of double-sided campus printing per semester.
